FINANCE POLICY: EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENT POLICY
Department: Finance
Last Reviewed: 2025-11-20
Policy Owner: Finance Controller Office

1. ELIGIBLE EXPENSES
Employees may claim reimbursement for business travel (flights, trains, taxis), client meals, accommodation, and approved business supplies. Personal expenses, alcohol (unless part of an approved client entertainment budget), and first-class travel are not reimbursable.

2. SUBMISSION DEADLINE
Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense being incurred, via the Expense Management System (EMS). Claims submitted after 30 days require Finance Director approval and may be rejected.

3. RECEIPT REQUIREMENTS
Original itemized receipts are required for any single expense over $25. Expenses under $25 may be submitted with a self-declaration but are capped at $100 total per month without receipts.

4. APPROVAL LIMITS
Expenses under $500 require manager approval only. Expenses between $500 and $5,000 require both manager and department head approval. Expenses exceeding $5,000 require Finance Director approval regardless of category.

5. TRAVEL BOOKING
All flights and hotels must be booked through the corporate travel portal to qualify for reimbursement, except in regions where the portal is unavailable, in which case pre-approval from Finance is required before booking externally.

6. REIMBURSEMENT TIMELINE
Approved claims are reimbursed within 10 business days via direct deposit to the employee's registered bank account.

For questions not covered here, contact finance-expenses@company.com.
