IT POLICY: PASSWORD AND DEVICE SECURITY POLICY
Department: Information Technology
Last Reviewed: 2026-02-01
Policy Owner: IT Security Team

1. PASSWORD REQUIREMENTS
All corporate account passwords must be a minimum of 14 characters and include at least one uppercase letter, one lowercase letter, one number, and one special character. Passwords must be changed every 180 days. Reuse of the last 10 passwords is not permitted. Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for all accounts accessing email, VPN, or SharePoint.

2. LAPTOP REQUEST PROCESS
New employees or employees requiring a laptop refresh must submit a request through the IT Service Desk portal. Standard laptop requests are fulfilled within 5 business days. Requests for non-standard hardware (e.g., high-performance workstations) require director-level approval and may take up to 15 business days.

3. LOST OR STOLEN DEVICES
Any lost or stolen corporate device must be reported to the IT Security Team within 2 hours of discovery via the emergency hotline (ext. 4357) or security@company.com. Failure to report within 24 hours may result in disciplinary action per the Acceptable Use Policy.

4. VPN AND REMOTE ACCESS
Remote access to internal systems requires the corporate VPN client and MFA. VPN sessions automatically time out after 8 hours of inactivity. Personal devices are not permitted to connect to internal systems except through the approved BYOD mobile app for email access only.

5. SOFTWARE INSTALLATION
Employees may not install unauthorized software on corporate devices. Requests for new software must go through the IT Service Desk and are reviewed for security and licensing compliance, typically within 3 business days.

For questions not covered here, contact it-helpdesk@company.com.
