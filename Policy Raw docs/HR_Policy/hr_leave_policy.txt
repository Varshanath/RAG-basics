HR POLICY: EMPLOYEE LEAVE POLICY
Department: Human Resources
Last Reviewed: 2026-01-15
Policy Owner: HR Business Partner Team

1. ANNUAL LEAVE
Full-time employees accrue 18 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, credited at 1.5 days per completed month of service. Annual leave must be requested at least 5 working days in advance via the HR portal and approved by the employee's manager. Unused annual leave can be carried forward up to a maximum of 10 days into the next calendar year; any balance beyond that is forfeited on March 31.

2. SICK LEAVE
Employees are entitled to 12 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for sick leave exceeding 2 consecutive working days. Sick leave does not carry forward and resets each January 1.

3. PARENTAL LEAVE
Primary caregivers are eligible for 26 weeks of paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers are eligible for 4 weeks of paid parental leave. Parental leave requests must be submitted at least 8 weeks before the expected leave start date, along with supporting medical documentation.

4. BEREAVEMENT LEAVE
Employees may take up to 5 days of paid bereavement leave for the death of an immediate family member (spouse, child, parent, sibling), and up to 2 days for extended family members.

5. UNPAID LEAVE OF ABSENCE
Employees with at least 1 year of tenure may request an unpaid leave of absence of up to 90 days, subject to manager and HR Business Partner approval. Job return is not guaranteed for unpaid leave exceeding 60 days.

For questions not covered here, contact hr-policies@company.com.
