PROCUREMENT POLICY: VENDOR ONBOARDING POLICY
Department: Procurement
Last Reviewed: 2026-03-05
Policy Owner: Procurement Operations

1. VENDOR REGISTRATION
All new vendors must complete registration in the Vendor Management Portal, including a signed W-9 (or local tax equivalent), banking details, and a completed Vendor Risk Questionnaire before any purchase order can be issued.

2. DUE DILIGENCE AND APPROVAL TIERS
Vendors with an expected annual spend under $10,000 require business-unit-level approval only. Vendors between $10,000 and $100,000 require Procurement and Legal review. Vendors exceeding $100,000 in expected annual spend require a full due diligence review, including financial health checks and a compliance/sanctions screening, which typically takes 10-15 business days.

3. CONTRACT REQUIREMENTS
No vendor engagement may begin without an executed Master Services Agreement (MSA) or purchase order signed by an authorized approver. Verbal agreements or email confirmations do not constitute an authorized engagement.

4. SOLE-SOURCE JUSTIFICATION
Any vendor selected without a competitive bid process (sole-source) requires a written justification memo approved by the Procurement Director, regardless of contract value.

5. VENDOR PERFORMANCE REVIEWS
Vendors with contracts exceeding $50,000 annually are subject to a quarterly performance review, coordinated by the business owner and Procurement.

6. OFFBOARDING
When a vendor relationship ends, the business owner must submit a Vendor Offboarding Form to ensure final invoices are settled and system access (if any) is revoked within 5 business days.

For questions not covered here, contact procurement-vendors@company.com.
